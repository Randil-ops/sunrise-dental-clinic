Help Section

1. Register New Appointment

Go to Register, fill in the patient's details, choose a treatment type, and pick a future date and time. The contact number must be exactly 10 digits. A unique appointment number (e.g. APT-0001) is generated automatically. The system will reject a booking if the same dentist already has an appointment at that exact date and time.

2. Display Appointment Details

Go to Search and enter the appointment number to view the full patient and appointment record.

3. Calculate and Print Bill

Go to Bill, enter the appointment number, and the system will calculate the total (treatment fee + clinic consultation fee) and mark the appointment as billed. Use the Print Receipt button to print it.

4. All Appointments

View every appointment currently on record, including whether it has been billed.

5. Logout / Exit

Use Logout in the navigation bar to safely end your session.
